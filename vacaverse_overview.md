# "Plan Apart. Play Together."

> “VacaVerse brings multi-generational families together through better vacation planning. Today's families are spread across different cities and states, which means planning a big family trip often falls to just one or two people trying to coordinate everyone through texts, emails, and different apps. We create a single place where the whole family can contribute to planning - from grandparents sharing their must-see spots to teenagers voting on activities. By making it easy for everyone to participate in planning, we help families focus on what matters most: creating memories together.”
> 

---

# Core Features

## Collaborative Trip Planning

Everyone can contribute to the itinerary from their own device, on their own time

## Visual Family Tree Builder

Fun and interactive way to build out your family

## Smart Budget Tracking

Real-time expense tracking with automatic cost-splitting across family members

## Centralized Booking Hub

One place for all confirmations, tickets, and reservations so nothing gets lost

## Shared Task Management

Assign trip prep tasks to different family members so the burden doesn't fall on one person

## Secure Document Sharing

Safe storage for passports, insurance, and travel documents that everyone can access

## Meal Planning & Grocery Coordination

Plan every meal, create shopping lists, and coordinate grocery delivery to your vacation rental for ultimate convenience

## Smart Document Reader

Upload confirmation emails, tickets, or PDFs and automatically extract booking details - saves time on manual data entry and ensures you never miss important details

## Activity Discovery & Voting

Find age-appropriate activities for everyone from toddlers to grandparents, with family voting so no one gets left out of the final itinerary

## Family Social Feed

Share updates, photos, and trip announcements with your travel group to keep everyone engaged and excited

## Multiple Family Groups

Create different family configurations (nuclear family, extended family, grandparents-only, etc.) for different types of trips and occasions
