# Vacaverse Landing Page Wireframe
## Brand-Integrated Cosmic Family Travel Experience

---

## Page Structure Overview

**Design Philosophy**: Mobile-first, conversion-optimized layout featuring "Modern Wanderlust" aesthetic with cosmic family constellation theme. Balances travel excitement with family trust while maintaining accessibility across all generations.

**Brand Integration**: Warm Cosmic Realism visual style with Cosmic Blue (#2B5CE6), Sunset Orange (#FF7B42), and Deep Space Navy (#1A2B5C) color palette. Typography: Poppins for headlines, Inter for body text.

**Target Conversion Goal**: App downloads and user signups  
**Primary User Journey**: Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Download  
**Cosmic Theme**: Family planning as orbital coordination - bringing scattered family members into perfect alignment
